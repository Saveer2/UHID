How it works

  1. Create your UHID

    Register once and receive a unique health identity.

  2. Connect your records

    Reports and images added by professionals appear in your timeline.

  3. Approve access

    Doctors and labs ask first. You choose what they see, and for how long.

Features

Virtual UHID card

A portable identity card you can show, copy and print.

Unified records

Reports, prescriptions and history gathered under one identity.

Image viewer

Open X-rays and scans reconstructed from the original file.

Lossless storage

Files shrink only when it is provably reversible, checked by SHA-256.

Patient-controlled access

Approve categories, set a duration, revoke at any time.

History and prescriptions

Every consultation and medicine in one timeline.

Access transparency

See who looked at what, and when.

Privacy

Knowing a UHID never opens a record. Every professional needs your approval, per category, with an expiry.
